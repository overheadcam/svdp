The Spirituality of the Home Visit

Before the logistics, the heart. A home visit is not a transaction — it is a spiritual encounter in which both the Neighbor and the Vincentian are meant to grow closer to Christ.

Our Charism — Why We Go

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul was founded in Paris in 1833 by Frederic Ozanam and a small group of fellow students who believed that faith must be put into action through direct, personal encounter with those who are poor. We take St. Vincent de Paul as our patron because of his tireless, hands-on service to the poor and sick of his own time.

We go two by two, as the disciples were sent — to listen, to accompany, and to serve, offering material help alongside genuine friendship, respect, and prayer.

“Lord, open our ears to hear this family as You hear them, and open our hearts to serve them as You would serve them. Let us bring Your peace into this home. Amen.”

Be Intentional About the Visit

Every visit deserves a moment of preparation of the heart, not just the checklist. Pause together on the doorstep. Remember whose home you are entering and whose work you are doing.

  • A hand up, not just a hand out
    Pair material aid with connection to resources that build lasting stability.
  • Serve with dignity, not judgment
    We are guests in the Neighbor’s home and in their story.
  • Confidentiality is sacred
    What is shared in a Neighbor’s home stays within the conference’s case records.
  • Generosity within stewardship
    Our goal is to maximize the impact and number of Neighbors served — not to conserve funds.
  • Honor silence
    It is not something to fill. Let the Neighbor tell their own story.

The Visit, in 9 Steps

  1. Pray before knocking. Take a moment together on the doorstep.
  2. Greet & introduce yourselves. Name your parish; show your SVdP ID.
  3. Listen — don’t interrogate. Let the Neighbor set the pace.
  4. Empathize — no promises yet. Reflect back what you hear.
  5. Determine our ability to assist. Reference this month’s limit.
  6. Make joint agency calls if needed. Include the Neighbor in the call.
  7. Fill out the Leave-Behind card. Document referrals and next steps.
  8. Pray together, if welcomed. Always an invitation, never an obligation.
  9. Thank them & explain next steps. Reaffirm that you’re glad they reached out.
“Lord, we thank You for the gift of this visit and for the trust of this family. Watch over their home, provide for their needs, and give them peace. We ask this through Christ our Lord. Amen.”
